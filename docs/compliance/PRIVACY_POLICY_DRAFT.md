# Privacy Policy Draft

Status: DRAFT_FOR_PROFESSIONAL_REVIEW

This draft is not effective and must be reviewed by privacy/legal professionals before publication.

## Controller and Contact

Product operator, company entity, registered address and privacy contact are pending business setup and legal review.

## Personal Information Processed

The current product may process account identity data, workspace membership data, ticket content, message content, invitation data, channel sender metadata, billing contact/order metadata and AI audit metadata.

## Purposes

Purposes include account registration, authentication, workspace collaboration, ticket handling, knowledge self-service, AI suggestion audit, entitlement enforcement, security, abuse prevention and customer support.

## Sharing and Third Parties

No production third-party payment, email, model, analytics, object storage or monitoring provider is connected in the current implementation. Future providers must be added to the third-party service list before production use.

## User Rights

Users may request access, correction, export, deletion, account cancellation and complaint handling through the support workflow after the workflow is legally reviewed and operationally staffed.

## Cross-Border Transfer

No production cross-border transfer is enabled. Any future model provider, cloud provider or support tool must be reviewed before customer data transfer.
