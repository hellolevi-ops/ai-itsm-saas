# User Agreement Draft

Status: DRAFT_FOR_PROFESSIONAL_REVIEW

This draft is not effective and must be reviewed by legal counsel before customer-facing use.

## Service Scope

Lingxi Service Desk provides workspace-based IT service management capabilities, including tickets, knowledge articles, mock AI assistance, mock channel integration, team invitations and manual commercial order records.

## Accounts and Workspaces

Users are responsible for accurate registration information and for protecting account credentials. Workspace owners are responsible for member invitations, role assignment and workspace data management.

## Acceptable Use

Users must not upload unlawful content, attack the service, bypass access controls, misuse AI suggestions, infringe third-party rights or process data without lawful authority.

## AI Assistance

AI outputs are suggestions only. Users remain responsible for review, correction and final operational decisions.

## Fees

Current M7 implementation supports manual/mock payment orders only. Real payment, invoice, tax, refund and renewal terms require professional review before production.

## Suspension and Termination

The service may suspend access for security risk, abuse, non-payment or legal requirement. Data return, export and deletion follow the draft retention policy after review.
