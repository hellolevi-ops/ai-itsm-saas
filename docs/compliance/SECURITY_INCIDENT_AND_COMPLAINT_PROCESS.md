# Security Incident and Complaint Process

Status: DRAFT_FOR_PROFESSIONAL_REVIEW

## Intake

Potential incidents and complaints may come from support tickets, email, monitoring, vulnerability reports, customer notices or internal staff.

## Triage

Classify by impact, affected workspace, personal information exposure, service availability, abuse risk and legal notification need.

## Response

1. Preserve evidence and request ids.
2. Contain the issue.
3. Assess affected users, data and systems.
4. Remediate and verify.
5. Communicate with customers as required after professional review.
6. Record root cause and prevention actions.

## Current Engineering Evidence

M8 added request id propagation and health probes. Production monitoring, alerting, incident staffing and legal notification workflow remain future work.
