# Data Processing Agreement Draft

Status: DRAFT_FOR_PROFESSIONAL_REVIEW

This draft identifies clauses that must be reviewed before enterprise customer use.

## Draft Clauses

- Roles of customer and service provider.
- Processing purpose, duration and data categories.
- Confidentiality and access control obligations.
- Subprocessor approval and notification.
- Security measures and incident notification.
- Assistance with user rights requests.
- Return, deletion and retention after service termination.
- Audit and compliance cooperation.
- Cross-border transfer restrictions if applicable.

The final agreement depends on operator entity, hosting model, third-party providers and customer segment.
