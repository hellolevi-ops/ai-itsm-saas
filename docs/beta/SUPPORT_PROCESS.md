# Customer Support Process

## Intake

During beta, support issues are captured through:

- `/beta` feedback form
- product interviews
- direct operator notes from test sessions

## Severity

- `CRITICAL`: blocks the full beta workflow or exposes data/security risk
- `HIGH`: blocks a major workflow with no practical workaround
- `MEDIUM`: affects workflow clarity or efficiency with workaround
- `LOW`: copy, polish or minor usability issue

## Triage

Daily during active beta:

1. Review open feedback.
2. Assign an owner.
3. Decide workaround, fix milestone or close reason.
4. Update release notes and known issues when customer-facing.

## Response Targets

- Critical: same day during active beta window
- High: next business day
- Medium/Low: next planned beta review

These are beta operating targets, not a production SLA.
